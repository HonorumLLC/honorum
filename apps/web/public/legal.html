Privacy Policy

Planning without an account

You can plan without signing in. Schedules, tasks, preferences, and downloaded catalogs are stored in your browser for offline use. Clearing your browser's site data removes those local copies. Honorum does not use third-party advertising, analytics trackers, or browser fingerprinting.

Accounts and notifications

If you sign in, we store your verified mobile number, an account identifier, device sessions, and the schedules, tasks, preferences, watches, and reminders you sync. Account documents are stored on the server as JSON. We also store your account time zone and, for each finished day, whether any synced task was left overdue, to calculate a shared streak. Visit dates recorded by earlier versions remain until you delete your account. Browser sign-in uses a session cookie. You can revoke devices or delete your account in Settings.

Seat alerts and reminders use push notification destinations and the relevant class or task details. You can remove seat alerts and turn off reminders in Settings.

SMS and consent

We send your mobile number to Twilio Verify to deliver and check a sign-in code. SMS frequency depends on the sign-in and resend requests you make. Standard message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell or rent mobile numbers or SMS consent information, or share them with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the app, deliver requested messages, prevent abuse, and meet legal requirements.

For each accepted code request, we record the submitted mobile number, the time you confirmed the request, and the version of the SMS notice. We retain these records for one year to document consent and investigate messaging issues, including after account deletion. A request record does not mean the number was verified or that a text was delivered. Sign-in codes and raw Twilio responses are not stored in our application logs.

Connected services and shared plans

When you connect Canvas while signed in, we store an encrypted access token with your account so all your devices can refresh assignments and send completion changes. Devices receive connection status and assignments, not the token. Disconnecting removes the stored credential and stops access across your account; existing assignments remain saved. Account calendar feed URLs also use encrypted server storage. The server imports their tasks for all your devices. Guest Canvas tokens and guest calendar feed URLs stay on the device where you entered them. They pass through our relay when used. Existing local Canvas connections are only uploaded when you choose to connect them to your account.

Google Calendar

When signed in, you can connect a Google account in either or both of two ways, and Google asks for each permission separately. Adding your classes lets Honorum create one calendar named Honorum in your Google account and keep it current with your schedules' classes, exams, and personal events and, if you choose, task due dates; Honorum can change only calendars it created. Showing Google events lets Honorum read the list of your calendars and the events on the ones you choose, from two weeks ago to four months ahead, so they appear on your week. Honorum stores each shown event's title, times, location, and link with your account and never changes events in your calendars.

We store your Google account's email address and an encrypted Google credential with your account. Devices receive the connection's status and the shown events, not the credential. Turning off adding classes deletes the Honorum calendar from Google. Hiding a calendar deletes our copies of its events. Disconnecting does both, revokes Honorum's access at Google, and deletes the stored credential. You can also remove Honorum's access in your Google Account settings.

Honorum's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to provide these features, never for advertising, we do not sell it, and people do not read it except when you ask for help with a problem, for security, or as the law requires.

Share links contain a secret that lets their recipients decrypt an uploaded schedule. The server stores its encrypted contents. Anyone with the complete link can access that copy, so share it only with people you intend to give access.

Infrastructure and retention

Cloudflare serves the web app and public catalogs. DigitalOcean hosts the backend, the PostgreSQL database, and its managed backups. Twilio processes phone verification. Push providers deliver notifications. These providers process the information needed to provide their services, which can include network addresses and delivery metadata. We use operational logs and temporary request limits to diagnose failures and reduce abuse. We keep daily totals, such as how many accounts exist or were active, for two years; they identify no one.

Deleting an account removes its active server account, sessions, synced documents, watches, reminders, Canvas credentials, Google Calendar connection and copied events, and streak history. Honorum also deletes its calendar from your Google account and revokes its access there. This device keeps its local plans. Signing out preserves a private local cache that returns when you sign back into that account. Browser site-data removal deletes local caches. Separate encrypted share copies and previously sent messages are not recalled by account deletion. Backups may retain earlier data until those backups expire or are removed.

You can export schedules from the app. Contact us to request help accessing or deleting your information.

Terms of Service

Using Honorum

Use Honorum for lawful planning and notification purposes. Only request a sign-in code for a mobile number you control. Do not send unsolicited messages, attempt to access another person's data, or interfere with the service. We may restrict access to prevent abuse.

Honorum is under active development and is provided as available. Features may change or be unavailable. Keep copies of plans you need. Course listings, seat counts, assignments, and notifications may be delayed or incomplete. Confirm registration, deadlines, and requirements with your school. Honorum does not register you for classes.

SMS sign-in terms

When you enter your mobile number and choose Send Code or Resend Code, you consent to receive a one-time transactional sign-in code from Honorum. Each code request authorizes a sign-in text. Frequency depends on your requests. These texts are for authentication; they do not enroll you in marketing or recurring SMS seat alerts.

Standard message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on Twilio, your carrier, and your device. Access to your phone number is required to sign in, and losing that number may mean losing access to your account.

To stop requesting texts, use Honorum without an account. You may also reply STOP to a received message where replies are supported. For help, reply HELP where supported or contact us at the email below. You do not need an account to use the local planner.

Changes

We will publish updates to these policies on this page with an effective date. A changed SMS consent notice requires a new confirmation when you request a code. These terms do not limit rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.
